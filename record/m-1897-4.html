      Marriage Date
      8 September 1897
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom (prose)
      Antsehel Wolf, called Wolf, Druck, a timber dealer born in Jaworze, No. 376, and settled there, son of Leib Druck, private, and Rivka, nee Freidman, in Jaworze
      Bride (prose)
      Matylda Wróbel, born in Brzostek at No. 111, the daughter of Aba Wróbel, the main trafficker in Brzostek, and Hannah, née Schaufel.

      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
