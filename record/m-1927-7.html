      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      21 June 1927
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Mechel Freund, single merchant in Korczyna, illegitimate son of Rosa Freund, merchant in Korczyna
      Bride
      Hencie Grabkowicz, single daughter of Salamon Grabkowicz and Ryfka nee Orner
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Lipa Rosner, schames in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 9 nov 1902, bride 1 sept 1896. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Korczyna, 31 Aug 1919, ref. no. 246, birth book there vol. VII, p. 120, no. 56; certificates of the banns dated Korczyna, 19 Jun 1927, ref. no. 5; also military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Sanok, of 2[4?] May 1925, Gr. ks. 2051, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 44, no. 31; and certificates of the rabbinate in Brzostek.
