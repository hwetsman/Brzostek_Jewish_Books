      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      6 April 1930
      Marriage Place
      Bukowa
      Groom
      Mojzesz Diamant, illegitimate son of Sara Diamant, born in Grodzisko, residing in Bukowa without a profession
      Bride
      Laja Diamant, recte Slanger, daughter of Isak Diamant, recte Slanger, farmer from Bukowa and Ruchla née Offner
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, Synagogue attendant from Brzostek and Hersch March, cantor from Brzostek
      Notes
      As for the groom, birth record dated Leżajsk, 1 Sep 1913, ref. no. 20[4?], vol. VI, p. ii, no. 84; also military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Sanok, Łańcut, gr. ew. 361, of 9 Feb 1928. As for the bride, birth record dated Frysztak, 17 Feb 1929, ref. no. 239, vol. IV, p. 214, no. 10; certificate of the banns, and notice of the rabbinate.
