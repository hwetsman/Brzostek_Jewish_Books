      Page scan: 1928_1-4.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      25 July 1928
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Chaim Markus, called Markus, Goldfarb, born in Wielopole and lives in Brzostek, son of Aron Samuel Goldfarb, merchant in Wielopole and Leji nee Meller
      Bride
      Jenta Mindla, called Mindla, Rubel, born and lives in Brzostek, daughter of Izak Rubel, farmer in Brzostek and Reisla nee Walter
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Mendel Schüss, owner of real estate in Brzostek, witness. Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 18feb 1899, bride 21 dec 1893. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Wielopole, 21 Mar 1927, according to the register there, vol. III, p. 115; military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Rzeszów, of 17 Mar 1923, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 21, no. 36; certificates of the banns, and certificates of the rabbinate.
