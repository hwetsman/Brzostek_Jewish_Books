      Page scan: 1922_5-7.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      7 September 1922
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Isak Moses, called Moses, Oling residing in Debica, son of Leiser Oling and Chaja Ita, called Ita, née Kukuk, merchant residing in Debica
      Bride
      Sara Ryfka, called Ryfka, Konigsdorf, without a job in Brzostek, daughter of Samuel Moses, called Moses, Konigsdorf and Muszkieta née Liebschutz, innkeepers in Brzostek
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Rubin Reich, merchant in Januszkowice, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 1896, bride 6 Sep 1891. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Dębica, 6 Aug 1922; certificate of the banns dated Dębica, 6 Aug 1922. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. II, p. 249, no. 20; certificate of the banns, and certificates of the rabbinate.
