      Transcription

      Translator's Note: Blima Kichel's birth is not listed in the books of Brzostek. Her existence and her parents are inferred from US immigration records. 17 Sep 1920 aboard SS Aquitania to Ellis Island, Blima Kichel age 25 single female tailor, listed father Leiser Kichel in Brzostek as next of kin. Age 25 in Sep 1920 → born ~Sep 1894 to Sep 1895. Joseph Kichel (b-1895-25, 22 Aug 1895) was confirmed same family and also emigrated (SS Hamburg 5 Feb 1914). It is assumed that Blima and Joseph were siblings but cannot both be born in 1895 by natural gestation — Blima likely b.late 1894 (older sister) or born in Biecz/Rzepiennik before the family settled in Brzostek (Leiser from Rzepiennik Strzyżewski, Esther from Biecz per m-1896-2)
