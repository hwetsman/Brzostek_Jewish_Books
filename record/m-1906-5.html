      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      12 April 1906
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom (prose)
      Gerschon Tesser, no profession, born in Brzeziny, son of Sasche and Yehudit Tesser, a tenant of a property in Brzeziny - however, he has been living in Wola Brzostecka for a dozen or so years
      Bride (prose)
      Chane Ezra, born in Wola Brzostecka, without profession and settled here, daughter of deceased Joseph Ezra, a former musician in Wola Brzostecka, and Hence née Wurzel
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Groom (summary)
      Gershon Tesser, age 44 years 3 months, no profession, born in Brzeziny, residing in Wola Brzostecka, single.
      Bride (summary)
      Hannah Ezra, age 40, without profession, born in Wola Brzostecka, residing in Wola Brzostecka, single.
      Groom's Father (summary)
      Sasche Tesser, tenant of a property, of Brzeziny.
      Groom's Mother (summary)
      Yehudit Tesser.
      Bride's Father (summary)
      Joseph Ezra, musician, of Wola Brzostecka, deceased.
      Bride's Mother (summary)
      Hence Ezra née Wurzel.
