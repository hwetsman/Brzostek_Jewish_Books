      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      16 March 1925
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Josef Schonwetter, without profession residing in Zawadka Brzostecka, son of Salamon Schonwetter, an inkeeper in Zawadka Brzostecka and Malki nee Amsterdam
      Bride
      Leja Gitla, called Gitla, Amsterdam, daughter of Abraham Amsterdam, grain dealer in Brzostek and Scheindli nee Lambek
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Moses Mayer Czopp, merchant in Brzostek, witness. Mendel Schüss, owner of an iron business in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 23 Dec 1894, bride 30 Oct 1899. Remarks: For the groom: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 30, no. 34; also military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Rzeszów, of 20 Oct[?] 1924, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 77, no. 36; certificates of the banns, and certificates of the rabbinate in Brzostek of 17 Mar 1925.
