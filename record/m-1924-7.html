      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      27 November 1924
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Israel Hillel, called Hillel, Freiberger, single, no occupation, son of Abraham Izak Freiberger, an innkeeper in Błaszkowa and Serla née Bakon
      Bride
      Ruchla Rothman, the widow of Neubart, residing in Brzostek, daughter of Samuel Dawid Rothman, a farmer from Gorzejowa, and Freidla, née Roth
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, synagogue attendant in Brzostek, witness. David Hirsch[...], private person in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 2 nov 1888, bride 2/may 1889. Remarks: For the groom: this registry's own birth book, vol. II, p. 232, no. 25; certificate of the commune board in Błażkowa of 18 Nov 1924 that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. II, p. 243, no. [1?]7; death record of [her] first husband dated Frysztak, 6 Nov 1924, ref. no. 219, vol. III, p. 219; certificates of the banns; certificates of the rabbinate. Below the entry: Closed as of 31 Dec 1924 [signature, illegible]; stamp of the Jewish Registrar in Brzostek.
