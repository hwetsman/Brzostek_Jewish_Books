      Marriage record 3 of 1907

      m-1907-3 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1907 · Number: 3

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1907

      Page scan: 1907_1-4_Diamand.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      17 March 1907
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Abraham Frommer, born in 1873, house No. 82 in the village of Żurawiczki ad Jarosław, without profession, son of Naftali Frommer, leaseholder of the liquor concession in Żurawiczki, and Hendli nee Sauerhaft
      Bride
      Ruchla Wróbel, born in Brzostek, without a profession, with her parents, daughter of Abe Wróbel, a tobacco storekeeper in Brzostek, and Chana née Schaufel
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Mendel Schuss, grain dealer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The groom's birth certificate dated Przeworsk, 17 May 1904, no. 94, vol. III, p. 32[?], no. 623; certificate of the banns dated Jarosław, 26 Mar 1907, no. 56; the military obligations fulfilled. For the bride, this registry's own birth book attests, vol. II, pp. 9–10, no. 19, of 1877; the banns announced, and the notice of the rabbinate.
