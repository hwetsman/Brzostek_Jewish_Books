      Marriage record 2 of 1912

      m-1912-2 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1912 · Number: 2

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1912

      Page scan: 1912_1-2.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      6 May 1912
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Mojzesz Peretz, called Peretz, Sperber, born in Siedliska, residing in Kamienica Dolna, owner of the property there and the American water mill, son of the deceased Kelman Sperber, former lessee of the propination in Siedliska, and Estera née Tager
      Bride
      Hinde daughter, born in Jodlowa, of Abraham Walter in Uniszowa ad Ryglice, farmer, and Sima née Kleinman
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, Schächter in Brzostek, witness. Salamon Aron, merchant of cloth goods in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom born in 1871 and bride 28 dec 1878. Remarks: Birth record, Dębowa, vol. II, p. 17, no. 18; assessment of the military tax by the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 10 Sep 18[95 or 97], ref. no. 1451; the banns duly announced, and the notice of the rabbinate. Below: Vidi [seen] Pilzno, 15 Jul 1912 [signature, illegible]; faint stamp: Head of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo).
