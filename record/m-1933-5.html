      Marriage Date
      3 October 1933
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Israel Schonwetter, son of the deceased Fischel Schonwetter, farmer in Brzostek and Złaty nee Wurzel
      Bride
      Sara Bein, daughter of the deceased Mendel Bein, farmer in Brzostek and Ruchli nee Berger
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, schames in Brzostek, witness. Abraham Barber, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 19 Feb 1904, bride 5 Apr 1905. Remarks: Documents of the groom: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 22, no. 4; divorce decision of the District Court in Brzostek of 14 Apr 1931, ref. no. Nc 232/31 [one digit possibly corrected to 3]. Documents of the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 148, no. 7; and the banns three times, and notices of the rabbinate. Below the entry: Closed as of 31 Dec 1933 [signature]. I certify that [this] agrees with the original. Jasło[?], 20 Jan 1934 [signature]; stamps: District Office (Starostwo Powiatowe) of Jasło; For the District Officer: Mgr. Nygrzynolski[?].
