      Marriage record 9 of 1927

      m-1927-9 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1927 · Number: 9

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1927

      Page scan: 1927_9.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      2 October 1927
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Abraham Sucher, called Sucher, Barber, born in Szczawnica, without profession, son of Lobel Barber, merchant in Szczawnica and Zine Ryfka, called Ryfka nee Bitterman
      Bride
      Chane Kleinman, daughter of Markus Kleinman, butcher in Brzostek and Chaja nee Grunberger
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Moses Amsterdam, merchant from Brzostek, witness. Israel Schönwetter, farmer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Krościenko, 26 Jan 1918, ref. no. 8, according to the book there, vol. I, p. 22[7?], no. 10; release card, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Nowy Sącz, 30 Sep 1921, ref. no. 762/900; certificate of the banns dated Krościenko, 12 Sep 1927, ref. no. 8. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. II, p. 32, no. 7; and certificates of the rabbinate in Brzostek. Below entry 9: Closed as of 31 Dec 1927 [signature]; stamp: Jewish Registrar in Brzostek.
