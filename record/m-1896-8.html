      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      15 January 1896
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Chaim David Wurst, called David, born in Brzezinach, House No. 171, son of Abraham Jakob Wurst, assistant surgeon in Brzezinach, and Risa Judah Lupper currently residing in Nizankowice
      Bride
      Meite Grabkowicz, born in Brzostek No. 2, daughter of Judah Grabkowicz, assistant surgeon in Brzostek and Chaja Sara née Weisenberg
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Jos.[?] Chaskel Leuchtag, private person in Brzostek, witness. David Töwel, private person, as schames in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: By order of the Honourable Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 29 Dec 1896, ref. no. 18393, this marriage record is entered late. Documents of the groom, namely: birth certificate of the registry district of Brzeżany[?], vol. IV, p. 92, no. 10; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Drohobycz, 14 Apr 1896, ref. no. 1827[?], that the military draft obligation has been fulfilled; also certificate of the banns; further, for the bride, the record of this registry district, vol. I, p. 10, no. 10[?]. Stamp: [S]TAROSTW[O], with a crowned eagle (partly cut off).
