      Transcription

      Translator's Note: The bride's mother's maiden name is in quotes here as the identified person has been attested as nee Schonwetter in numerous other entries.
      Marriage Date
      19 January 1902
      Marriage Place
      Blaszkowa
      Groom
      Schulem Schonwetter, born in Zawadka house no. 2, helps his father in the agricultural farm in Zawadka, son of Leiba Schönwetter, owner of the estate in Zawadka and Chana nee Korzennik
      Bride
      Ryfke Dintenfass, born in Zawadka house No. 23, daughter of Mendel Dintenfass, who rented a farm in Blaszkowa, and Estera née Korzennik
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Chaim Galizer, Schächter in Brzostek, witness. Salomon Abraham, owner of an iron business in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The capacity for the marriage entered alongside is attested by this registry's own books, namely for the groom the birth book, vol. I, p. 12, no. 134, of [18]75, and for the bride the birth book, vol. II, p. 123, no. 27, of [18]82; also the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate; finally[?] the consent of the legitimate father, with his own [signature; word not written], to the contracting of this [one word struck through] legal marriage of [his] minor[?] daughter[?]. [Signed:] Mendel Dintenfass.
