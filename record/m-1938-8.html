      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      2 August 1938
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Joel Klein, farmer in Góra Motyczna near Debica
      Bride
      Cyrla Fichner, daughter of Mojzesz Fichner, farmer in Błaszkowa and Chaja nee Korzenik
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Abraham Barber, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Herz Parnes, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      grrom 1903, bride 18 dec 1908. Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate dated Dębica, 8 Jun 1938, ref. no. 126, without the parents' names given; certificate of the banns dated Dębica, 14 Jun 1938. Documents of the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 159, no. 27; the banns three times, and notices of the rabbinate in Brzostek of 4 Aug 1938.
