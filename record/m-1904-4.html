      m-1904-4 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1904 · Number: 4

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1904

      Page scan: 1904_4-5.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Translator's Note: The groom's mother's maiden name is left off the record and this is the only place his parents appear.
      Marriage Date
      15 April 1904
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Hersch Bieder, born and residing in Brzostek, unemployed, son of Blessed Israel Bieder, former shemes in Brzostek, and Maryem nee (mother's maiden name missing)
      Bride
      Itte Rieger, born in Gorlice, daughter of Blessed Leiba and Perla Rieger, former industrialists in Gorlice
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, Schächter in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The capacity for the marriage entered alongside rests on [the registrar's] own knowledge, and also on the bride's birth certificate in Gorlice, vol. III, p. 85, no. 79, of 28 Jun 1900, no. 178; the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate. Below: Checked 17 Jun 1904 [signature, Pola?/Pole?], Imperial-Royal district commissioner[?].
