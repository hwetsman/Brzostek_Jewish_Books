      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      16 January 1935
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Fischel Goldman, born in Nagoszyn ad Debica and lives in Brzostek, merchant, son of Aron Goldman, former leaseholder of tolls and Tauba nee Drobner
      Bride
      Malle nee Steinbok, widow of Josef Schmidt, born in Lezajsk and lives in Brzostek, illegitimate daughter of Golda Steinbok
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Pinkas Kasten, merchant in Brzostek, witness. Abraham Barber, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 17 Jun 1884, bride 11 Oct 1893. Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate in Dębica, according to[?] the birth book there of the year 1884, p. 13, no. 65; court file on the death (akta zejścia) of the District Court in Brzostek, ref. no. A. 161/30, that [his] first wife Gitla née Plattner died. Documents of the bride: record of [her] first marriage dated Leżajsk, 6 Sep [19]14, ref. no. 195; and court file on the death (akta zejścia) of the District Court in Przemyśl of 2 Oct 1930, ref. no. V. A. 129/3[8?], that [her] first husband Josef[?] Schmidt died on 10 Dec; and the banns three times, and [notices of] the rabbinate in Brzostek.
