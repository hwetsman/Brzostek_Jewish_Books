      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      22 March 1928
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Wolf Leib Pancer, butcher, son of Moses Pancer, butcher in Brzostek and Gitla nee Beil
      Bride
      Chana List, daughter of Salomon Selmon List, egg merchant in Brzostek and Tyle nee Rubel, recte Korzenik
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, synagogue attendant in Brzostek, witness. Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom April 1902, bride Feb 1901. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Jasienica, 1 May 1922, ref. no. 39, according to the birth register there, vol. III, p. 121, no. 12; also military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Rzeszów, of 1 Jun 1926, ref. no. 709, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 96; certificates of the banns, and certificate of the rabbinate in Brzostek of 1 Apr 1928.
