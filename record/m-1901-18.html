      Groom (prose)
      Abraham Isaac, called Isaac, Freiberger, born and residing in Blaszkowa, innkeeper, son of the late Leiser Freiberger, a former innkeeper in Blaszkowa, and Freida, nee Fornat
      Bride (prose)
      Serla Bakon, born in Podgrodzie, residing in Blaszkowa without employment, daughter of the blessed Israel Hillel Bakon, former furrier in Siedliska, Imperial-Royal Starostwo Mielec, and Sarah Mindla, called Mindla, Bakon
