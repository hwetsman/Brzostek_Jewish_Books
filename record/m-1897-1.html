      Groom (prose)
      Michal Finder, born in Rudka ad Wieckowice, Imperial-Royal Brzesko district, owner of the estate and resident here, son of Mendel and Freida Finder, owners of a land holding and alcohol production monopoly in Rudka ad Wieckowice, Imperial-Royal Brzesko district
      Bride (prose)
      Henryka, recte Hinde, Kornhauser, born in Wielka Wies, Imperial-Royal, starosty of Brzesko, co-owner of the leather estate and residing here, daughter of Mayer and Rivka Kornhauser, deceased, former tenants of the Wieckowice estate, Imperial-Royal, starosty of Brzesko.
