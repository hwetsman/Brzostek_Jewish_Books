      Marriage Date
      30 December 1908
      Marriage Place
      Blaszkowa
      Groom (prose)
      David Wistreich, false Altholz, born in Chlebna, illegitimate of his mother, son of the late Hersch Altholz, a former propinator in Chlebna, and Freida, nee Wistreich
      Bride (prose)
      Hence Sussla, called Sussla, Dintenfass, born in Zawadka Brzostecka, daughter of Mendel Dintenfass, a tenant of a manor in Blaszkowa, and Esther, née Schonwetter
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld

      Groom's Father (summary)
      Hersch Altholz, propinator, of Chlebna, deceased.
      Groom's Mother (summary)
      Freida Altholz née Wistreich.
      Bride's Father (summary)
      Mendel Dintenfass, tenant of a manor, of Blaszkowa.
      Bride's Mother (summary)
      Esther Dintenfass née Schonwetter.
