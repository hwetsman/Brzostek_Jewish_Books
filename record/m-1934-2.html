      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      18 February 1934
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Efraim Licht, merchant in Brzostek, son of the deceased Lazar Licht, merchant in Brzostek and Debora nee Plattner
      Bride
      Alta Sara Chana, called Chana, Silber, daughter of Herscha Silber, merchant in Rozwadów and Dyny nee Silber
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Abraham Barber, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Naftali Herz Parnes, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      22 April 1896, bride 10 Sep 05. Remarks: Documents of the groom: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 73, no. 17. Documents of the bride: birth certificate dated Rozwadów, 20 Mar 1917, according to the birth book there, p. 76; also certificate of registration [of residence] of the commune office in Brzostek of 16 Sep 1932; and the banns three times, and notices of the rabbinate in Brzostek.
