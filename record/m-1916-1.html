      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      30 January 1916
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Chaim Symche, called Symche, Weller, born in Gogolów on 19/1/1891, son of Dawid Weller, a laborer in Gogolow and Rojza née Haber, without profession, residing in Gogolów
      Bride
      Feiga Kleinman, the illegitimate daughter of Chaja Kleinman, who has no profession, lives in Januszkowice
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Hirsch March, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Documents of the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III[?], p. 4; decree of the Imperial-Royal District Court in Brzostek of 9 Mar 1914, ref. no. P 13/14, declaring her of full age. Of the groom: birth certificate dated Frysztak, 27 Jan 1916; militia (Landsturm) identity card dated Strzyżów, 13 Jun 1915, that he has fulfilled the military obligations; certificate of the banns, and certificate of the rabbinate.
