      Marriage record 11 of 1907

      m-1907-11 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1907 · Number: 11

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1907

      Page scan: 1907_9-11.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      27 October 1907
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Israel Bieder, called Kornreich, born and residing in Brzostek, a master shoemaker, son of Leizer Dawid Kornreich, without profession, and the deceased Debora, nee Bieder
      Bride
      Feige Rothman, born in Gorzejowa, daughter of the deceased Samuel Dawid, called Dawid, Rothman, a former innkeeper in Gorzejowa, and Freidla, née Roth
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Mojzes Fichner, dealer in basket rods in Błażkowa, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: As to the birth of the newly-weds, this registry's own book attests, vol. II, pp. 95–6, no. 16, of [18]81, and vol. II, pp. 193–4, no. 31, of [18]86; the groom belongs to the reserve; the banns announced as prescribed, and the notice of the rabbinate; and likewise, for the bride, the permission of the superior guardianship authority of 27 Oct. At the foot of the right-hand page: Vidi [seen] 20 Jan 1908 [signature, illegible]. At the foot of the left-hand page: Concluded and signed on 31 Dec 1907 [signature and title, cut off at the edge of the photo].
