      Marriage record 9 of 1900

      m-1900-9 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1900 · Number: 9

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1900

      Page scan: 1900_9-10.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      10 July 1900
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Leib Schneps, born in Podgrodzie, miller, tenant of the mill in Januszkowice and there residing, son of Mojżesz Schneps, a former innkeeper in Podgrodzie, and Chaja Ruchla, called Ruchla, nee Miller
      Bride
      Malka Schneps, born in Podgrodzie, daughter of Uscher Chaim, called Chaim, Schneps, a farmer in Podgrodzie, and Sara Laja, called Laji née Schächter.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Birth certificate for the groom dated Dębica, 26 Jun 1900, ref. no. 106, vol. I, p. 5, no. 14, and the banns announced three times; for the bride, [the registrar's] own knowledge, under § 32 of the register instructions.
