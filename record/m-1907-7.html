      30 June 1907
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom (prose)
      Israel Reiner, born in Debica and residing there, industrialist, son of the deceased Chaim Reiner, who was a bookbinder there and the deceased Chaya Sarah, called Sarah, Reiner
      Bride (prose)
      Sprinza Schachter, without profession, born in Ryglice and settled there, daughter of the deceased Wolf and the deceased Esther Schachter, former industrialists in Ryglice
