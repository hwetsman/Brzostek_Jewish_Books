      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      2 January 1926
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Chaskel Klausner, illegitimate son of Scheindli Klausner, born in Mielcu and resides in Wola Brzostecka without profession
      Bride
      Pese Aron, daughter of Salamon Dawid, called Dawid, Aron, owner of a dry good store in Brzostek I Malki Perli, called Perl, nee Tager
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Walter Mojzesz, merchant in Brzostek, witness. Jakob Schwarz, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 6 March 1899, bride 13 dec 1898 | Later annotation (rescript of the Kraków voivode, 30 Jan 1930): the groom was legitimated by the subsequent marriage of his parents Samuel Turn and Scheindla Klausner and, as Samuel Turn's legitimate son, is entitled to the surname Turn. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Mielec, 28[?] Sep 1920, ref. no. 37[1?]/20, according to the birth register there, vol. XIII, p. 10, no. 36; also military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Rzeszów, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 66, no. 32; certificate of the banns, and also certificates of the rabbinate in Brzostek of 3 Jan 1926.
