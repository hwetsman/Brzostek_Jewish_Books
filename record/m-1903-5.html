      Marriage record 5 of 1903

      m-1903-5 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1903 · Number: 5

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1903

      Page scan: 1903_5-6.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      21 August 1903
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Joel Panzer, called Rubel, born in Brzostek, at No. 118, unemployed, illegitimate son of Dawid Leib, called Leib, Rubel, an egg merchant in Brzostek, and Eiga, nee Panzer
      Bride
      Rivka Schonwetter, called Fisch, born in Toki near Nowy Zmigród, unemployed, illegitimate daughter of Berl Fisch, owner of a real estate in Toki, and Eidla, née Schonwetter
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: This registry's own book, vol. II, pp. 39–40, no. 29, of [18]78; military obligations fulfilled, as to the groom; also birth certificate for the bride dated Żmigród, 24 Aug 1902, ref. no. 130, vol. II, pp. 213–14, no. 63; the father's written consent to this marriage, dated Toki, 6 Aug 1903; further, the banns duly announced, and the notice of the rabbinate.
