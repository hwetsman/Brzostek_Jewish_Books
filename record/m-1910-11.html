      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      12 February 1910
      Marriage Place
      Grudna Gorna
      Groom
      Josef Mahrer, born in Jodłowa, without profession, son of Szymon Mahrer, industrialist in Jodłowa, and Ryfka, nee Siegfried
      Bride
      Pesla Rosenbaum, born in Gumniska ad Debica, without a profession, with her parents, daughter of Izaak Rosenbaum, a tavern keeper in Grudna Gorna, and Scheindl, née Wurzel
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Moses Klotz, owner of a mixed-goods business in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 6 Oct 1886 and bride 12 Sep 1889. Remarks: Late entry made on the basis of the permission of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 30 Nov 1910, ref. no. 21900. The documents of the groom and the bride for this marriage are in order and kept in the register files. The parents of the couple permit the marriage.
