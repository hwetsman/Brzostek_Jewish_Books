      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      1 September 1922
      Marriage Place
      Blaszkowa
      Groom
      Dow Mantel, no profession, residing in Błaszkowa, son of Israel Jakob Mantel and Ruchla Malka née Reiss, residing in Ropczyce
      Bride
      Ryfke Freiberger, without a profession in Błaszkowa, daughter of Abraham Isak Freiberger, an innkeeper in Błaszkowa, and Serla née Bakon
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Rubin Reich, merchant in Januszkowice, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 11 jan 1898, bride 1886. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Ropczyce, 16 Jun 1922; certificate of residence from the commune office in Błażkowa of 1 Aug 1922; demobilisation card of the District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) in Rzeszów of 9 Jun 1921, ref. no. 889/43/21[?], that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. II, p. 189, no. 23; certificate of the banns; certificates of the rabbinate.
