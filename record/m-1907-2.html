      Marriage record 2 of 1907

      m-1907-2 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1907 · Number: 2

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1907

      Page scan: 1907_1-4_Diamand.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      5 March 1907
      Marriage Place
      Opacionka
      Groom
      Dawid Wenig, born in Rymanów, without a profession, son of Baruch Wenig, owner of real estate in Rymanów and Ryfka, nee Mozes
      Bride
      Genendel Karpf, born in Łęki ad Frysztak, daughter of Salamon Karpf, owner of the estate in Opacionka and Perla née Diamant
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Moses Schwarz, grain dealer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The groom's birth certificate dated Rymanów, 1[3 or 5] Jan 1907, ref. no. 17, vol. III, p. 46, no. 59; certificate of the banns dated Rymanów, 27 Feb 1907, no. 57; proof that the military obligation has been fulfilled; the bride's birth certificate dated Frysztak, 17 Jan 1907, ref. no. 29, vol. II, p. 8[6 or 8], no. 7; the banns announced, and the notice of the rabbinate, with the added[?] signature of the father permitting this marriage. Salamon Karpf [signature].
