      m-1910-2 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1910 · Number: 2

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1910

      Page scan: 1910_1-2_Bodner.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      5 March 1910
      Marriage Place
      Nawsie Brzosteckie
      Groom
      Selman Bodner, born in Jazowy, royal and imperial district of Strzyzow, without profession, son of Heisig Bodner, propination leaseholder in Jazowy, and Ryfka nee Tratner.
      Bride
      Beile Langer, daughter of the deceased Hersch Langer, born in Nawsie Brzosteckie and Chana née Herman, the latter's parents were butchers in Nawsie Brzosteckie
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Izak Rewitz, owner of a mixed-goods stall in Nawsie Brzosteckie, witness. Mojżes Roth, cattle dealer in Januszkowice, witness.
      Notes
      groom born on 9 Oct 1884 and bride on 14 Oct 1889. Remarks: Birth record [one short word struck through] for the groom, Frysztak, vol. II, p. 12, no. 34; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Strzyżów of 31 Mar 1910, ref. no. 4339, that the military obligation has been fulfilled; certificate of the banns. For the bride, this registry's own birth book attests, vol. II, p. 247, no. 25, and her full age, recognised by decree of the Imperial-Royal Court in Brzostek of 25 Feb 1910, ref. no. [one letter unclear] 19/8; and the notice of the rabbinate; in the register files.
