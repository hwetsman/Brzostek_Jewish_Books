      Marriage Date
      22 December 1901
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom (prose)
      Moses Mayer Czopp, called Mayer, born in Dukla, teacher of the Jewish religion in Brzostek and there residing, son of Ephraim Czopp, a bookkeeper in Dukla, and Shayndel, nee Welisch
      Bride (prose)
      Reisla Indyk, who used the last name Ryf, born in Dukla and living in Brzostek, daughter of Leib Ryf, industrialist in Stary Lupkow, and Hene nee Indyk
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
