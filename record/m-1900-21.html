      Marriage record 21 of 1900

      m-1900-21 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1900 · Number: 21

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1900

      Page scan: 1900_21-22_Josef_Offner.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      26 October 1900
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Jozef Offner, born in Bukowa and residing here, tenant of the manor farm there, son of deceased parents Eisik Offner, former innkeeper in Bukowa, and Nichy nee Meth
      Bride
      Chaja Krautblatt, born in Cieklin near Żmigród, residing in Bukowa near the farmstead, daughter of deceased parents Chiel Krautblatt, a former farmer in Cieklin and Sara née Salamon
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Marcus Kleinman, sworn schochet in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, sworn schochet in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Capacity to contract the present marriage rests on [the registrar's] own conviction, together with the threefold announcement of the banns and the notification of the rabbinate
