      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      15 August 1927
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Hersch Orner, single merchant born in Brzostek and living in Wroclaw, son of Leib Orner, cattle dealer in Brzostek and Ryfka nee Blasbalg
      Bride
      Feiga Taube, called Taube, Rossner, single, born in Tarnow 14 Jun 1894 and there resides, daughter of Wolf Rossner, a dealer in ready made clothes in Tarnow and Rachela nee Fleischer
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Mechel Freund, without occupation in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Panzer, butcher in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 23 nov 1892 bride 14 jun 1894. Remarks: For the groom: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 9, no. 35; military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Rzeszów, of 9 Jan 1926, ref. no. 175, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]; confirmations of single status from the Polish Consulate in Wrocław (Breslau) of 6 Aug 1927, no. 825/o[?]III[?], and from the Police Presidium in Wrocław of 28 Jul 1927, ref. no. [?]/08, and from the Rabbinate in Wrocław; also certificates of the banns. For the bride: birth certificate dated Tarnów, 3 Feb 1926, year 1894, p. 237, no. 247; certificates of the banns, Tarnów, of 24 Jul 1927; and notices of the Rabbi in Brzostek.
