      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      25 March 1924
      Marriage Place
      Zawadka Brzostecka
      Groom
      Hirsch Schonwetter, single, no profession in Zawadka, son of Salamon Schonwetter, farmer in Zawadka and Malka née Amsterdam
      Bride
      Hinde Schonwetter, single, daughter of Fischl Schonwetter, a farmer in Brzostek, and Złata, née Würzel
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Chaskel Goldberg, owner of real estate in Brzostek. Peretz Sperber, owner of the mill in Kamienica Dolna.
      Notes
      groom 21 nov 1891, bride 18 Aug 1896. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Mielec, 4 Jan 1924, ref. no. 63/24, according to the birth register there, vol. IX, p. 118, no. 163; also military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Rzeszów, 19 Dec 1923. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 45, no. 28; and certificates of the banns, and certificates of the rabbinate.
