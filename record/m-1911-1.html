      m-1911-1 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1911 · Number: 1

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1911

      Page scan: 1911_1-4_Offner.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      3 August 1911
      Marriage Place
      Gorzejowa
      Groom
      Leiser Walter, born in Ryglice, no profession, residing in Gorzejowa, son of Szyja Walter, a farmer in Ryglice and Malka née Steinhorn
      Bride
      Chana Kleinman, born in Kamienica Dolna, daughter of Kelman Kleinman, a butcher in Kamienica Dolna, and Jerla, née Baster [sic, = Basler]
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Birn, owner of real estate and tobacconist in Siedliska, witness. Markus Kleinman, Schächter in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 27 Feb 1883 bride 20 Sep 1891. Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate, Tuchów, 4 Jan [18]90, vol. I, pp. 93–4, no. 22; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Tarnów of 18 Jul 1911 that the military obligation has been fulfilled. For the bride, this registry's own book attests, vol. II, pp. 281–2, no. 22; with the father's signature, as permitting this marriage. Kelman Kleinman [signature].
