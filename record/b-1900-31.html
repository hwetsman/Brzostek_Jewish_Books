      Notes
      Pursuant to the instruction of the Governor's Office in Lwow dated 22 April 1921, file no. 50179, and of the District Office in Pilzno dated 20 April 1921, file no. 6348/4, the following is additionally entered: Esther, the legitimate daughter of Lazar Licht and Deborah nee Kainer, born in Brzostek in the year 1900. The marriage record of this child's parents is recorded in the Israelite metrical district in Brzostek for the year 1880, Volume II, page 9, entry no. 2. Brzostek 9May21 "Wilhelm Zahler", keeper of the Israelite vital records
