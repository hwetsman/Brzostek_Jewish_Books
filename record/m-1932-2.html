      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      2 April 1932
      Marriage Place
      Kamienica Dolna
      Groom
      Salamon Leib, called Leib, Kleinman, merchant in Krosno, son of Lipy Kleinman, merchant in Krosno and Rechli nee Stern
      Bride
      Syme Sperber, without profession, daughter of the deceased Mojzesz Peretz Sperber, former miller in Kamienica Dolna and Hindy nee Walter
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, schames in Brzostek, witness. Mendel Schüss, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 22 march 1908, bride 20 apr 1905. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Krosno, 28 Feb 1932, ref. no. 27, according to the book there, vol. I, p. 184, no. 22; certificate of the banns dated Krosno, 13 Mar 1932, ref. no. 4; military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Sanok, ref. no. 470. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 129, no. 13; certificate of the banns, and notices of the rabbinate.
