      Marriage record 5 of 1899

      m-1899-5 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1899 · Number: 5

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1899

      Page scan: 1899_4-5.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      22 November 1899
      Marriage Place
      Gebiczyna
      Groom
      Jakob Blut, son of Chaim and Selda, born in Ropczyce, residing in Gębiczyn, owner of real estate and industrialists there
      Bride
      Ruchla Balsam, daughter of Aron and Süssla, born in Ropczyce, lives in Gebiczyna and helps Jakob Blut on the farm
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The capacity to contract this marriage rests on [the registrar's] own knowledge, under § 32 of the register instructions, and also on the ruling of the High Imperial-Royal Ministry of 22 Aug 1887, ref. no. 12262; further, on the certificate of the commune authority in Gębiczyna of 20 Nov 1899; and the banns announced three times. At the foot of the page: Closed on 31 Dec 1899 [signature, partly cut off: A. Wróbel?].
