      m-1902-2 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1902 · Number: 2

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1902

      Page scan: 1902_1-3.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Translator's Note: In the groom's description, the scribe originally wrote Leib Meer as the father and then another pen or pencil scratched out Leib and wrote the genitive name Jojny which I have matched to Jonas Meer
      Marriage Date
      22 January 1902
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Leib Meer, born and living in Głobikówka, innkeeper, son of Jojna Meer, a former innkeeper there, and Matla, nee Friedman
      Bride
      Hudesa Kranz, called Wurzel, born in Głobikówka, daughter of the deceased Wolf Wurzel, a former innkeeper in Głobikówka, and Pesla, née Kranz.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Chaim Galizer, Schächter in Brzostek, witness. Moses Mayer Czopp, keeping a cheder in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The capacity to contract the marriage entered alongside rests on [the registrar's] own knowledge, under § 32 of the register instructions; the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate.
