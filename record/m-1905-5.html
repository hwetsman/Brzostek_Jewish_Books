      Marriage record 5 of 1905

      m-1905-5 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1905 · Number: 5

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1905

      Page scan: 1905_4-5.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      11 September 1905
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Mendel Bein, born in Gorajowice ad Jasło, practising the butcher's trade in Brzostek, residing in Siedliska, son of Wolf Bein and Ruchla nee Meer
      Bride
      Ruchla Berger, born in Brzeziny, unemployed, daughter of Izaak Berger, owner of a land farm in Brzeziny and Rechla, née Berger
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Certificate dated Frysztak, 23 Apr 1903, and likewise the militia (Landsturm) passport for the groom dated Rzeszów, 26 May 1900, no. 55; further, certificate of the commune office in Brzeziny of 4 Sep 1905, ref. no. 175, for the bride; also the banns duly announced, and the notice of the rabbinate. Below: Vidi [seen] 10 Jan 1906 [signature, Pole/Pola], Imperial-Royal district commissioner.
