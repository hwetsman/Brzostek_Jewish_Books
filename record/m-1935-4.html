      m-1935-4 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1935 · Number: 4

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1935

      Page scan: 1935_1-4.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      1 October 1935
      Marriage Place
      Januszkowice
      Groom
      Moses Dawid, called Dawid, Tugendhoft, born and lived in Ulanów, single, son of Saul, snd Schejwy nee Feit, Tugendhoft from Ulanów
      Bride
      Maryem Judesa, called Judesa, Lobel, born and lives in Januszkowice, daughter of Markus Lobel and Malka nee Schlanger in Januszkowice
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Herz Parnes, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Abraham Barber, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 22 Dec 1903, bride 14 Apr 1907. Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate dated Ulanów, 1[0?] Sep 1935, ref. no. 117/35 [struck out: Documen…]; certificate of the banns dated Ulanów, 2 Oct 1935, ref. no. 117/35. Documents of the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 145, no. 12; and the banns three times, and notices of the rabbinate in Brzostek.
