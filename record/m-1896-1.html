      Page scan: 1896_1-4.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      1 January 1896
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Schaja Baumgarten, born in Korczyna, living with his parents, son of Eisik Baumgarten, a merchant in Korczyna, and Breidla, nee Fessel
      Bride
      Hudesa Hirsch, born in Brzostek No. 52, in her parents' family, daughter of Markus Dawid, called Dawid, Hirsch, a grain merchant in Brzostek, and Hendla née Sperber.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Alter Ungarischer, owner of a cloth stall in Brzostek, witness. Salamon Guttenberg, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Krosno of 4 Aug 1894, ref. no. 12051; certificate of the registry office in Korczyna of 11 Dec 1894, ref. no. 124; certificate of the banns; for the groom. For the bride, this registry's own book, vol. I, p. 6, no. 51, shows the capacity to contract a legal marriage.
