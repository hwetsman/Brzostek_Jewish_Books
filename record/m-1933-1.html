      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      28 February 1933
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Hersz Rubin, born in Tarnow and there resides, son Chaim Rubin and Chaji nee Weisman in Tarnow
      Bride
      Amalja Kleinhandler, born in Tarnow and there resides, daughter of Naftalego Kleinhandler and Jani nee Koplik in Tarnow
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Pinkas Kasten, in Brzostek, witness. Abraham Heringfass, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 25 Mar 1907, bride 10oct 1898. Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate dated Tarnów, 1[7?] Oct 1925, according to the birth book there, 1907, vol. II[?], no. 19; certificate of domicile in Tarnów, ref. no. 20035. Documents of the bride: certificate of the commune of Tarnów of 17 Feb 1933, ref. no. 63; certificate of domicile dated Tarnów, 6 Jul 1917, no. 155/17; authorisation by the rabbinate in Tarnów empowering the rabbinate in Brzostek to perform the marriage, dated Tarnów, 17 Feb 1933; and the official notices of the rabbinate in Brzostek.
