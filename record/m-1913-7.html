      Marriage record 7 of 1913

      m-1913-7 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1913 · Number: 7

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1913

      Page scan: 1913_7_Offner_Leibel.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      17 August 1913
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Mordko Brand, born in Dąbrowa 5/3 1890, no profession, son of Michal Brand, communal secretary in Dąbrowa, and his mother Chana, née Offen
      Bride
      Beila Zahler, born in Brzostek on 6/12/1890, no profession, daughter of Wilhelm Zahler, director of (something) advance payment in Brzostek, and Maria née Leibel
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Leib Faust, merchant in Brzostek, witness. Kelman Birnbaum, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate dated Dąbrowa, 10 Aug 1910; decree of the Imperial-Royal District Court in Dąbrowa of 13 May 1913, ref. no. Nc I 312[?]/13, declaring him of full age; and resolution of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Dąbrowa of 13 May 1913 that he has fulfilled the military obligations. For the bride: this registry's own birth book; decree of the Imperial-Royal District Court in Brzostek of 25 Jun 1913, Nc I 210/13, declaring her of full age; certificate of the banns, and certificate of the rabbinate. Below the entry: Closed as of 31 Dec 1913 [1914 written first, corrected]. Wilhelm Zahler [signature]; stamp: Jewish Registrar in Brzostek. Slanting: I certify that the duplicate agrees with the original. Pilzno, 17 Jan 1914[?] [signature], Imperial-Royal district commissioner[?].
