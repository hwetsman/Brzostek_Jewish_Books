      m-1906-9 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1906 · Number: 9

      Page scan: 1906_7-9.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Translator's Note: Hene Orner's maiden name is listed as both Licht and Luft in more than one record. Her father is never mentioned and there is no way to know which name was her fathers and her mothers.
      Marriage Date
      5 December 1906
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Salamon Grabkowicz, born in Brzostek, running Jewish schools and residing in Brzostek, son of deceased Juda Grabkowicz, a former barber in Brzostek, and Chaja Sara, called Sara, nee Weisenberg
      Bride
      Ryfka Orner, born in Brzostek, unemployed, daughter of Abe Orner, a baker in Brzostek, and the deceased Hene, née Luft
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The capacity for the marriage contracted alongside rests on [the registrar's] own knowledge, under § 32 of the register instructions; the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate.
