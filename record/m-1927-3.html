      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      17 January 1927
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Meilech Nurnberg, merchant w Strzyzów, son Chaim Jakob Nurnberg, merchant in Strzyzów n Wislokiem and Reisla nee Weinberg
      Bride
      Maryem Schwarz, daughter of Mojzesz Schwarz, merchant in Brzostek and Malki nee Rubel
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, synagogue attendant in Brzostek, witness. Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Strzyżów, 3 Jan 1927, ref. no. 1, according to the register there, vol. IV, p. 176, no. 30; certificates of the banns dated Strzyżów, 3 Jan 1927; military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Sanok, of 6 Aug 1924, ref. no. 468, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 67, no. 30; certificates of the banns, and certificates of the rabbinate.
