      Page scan: 1900_5-6.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      4 July 1900
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Jacob Littman, born in Dukla, itinerant pedlar residing in Brzostek, son of Süsche Littman, industrialist in Dukla and Jochweta nee Atlas
      Bride
      Freida Schonwetter, born in Wielopole Skrzyńskie, resides in Brzostek, daughter of Mendel Schonwetter, tailor, from Wielopole Skrzyńskie, and Elka née (mother's maiden name missing).
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Birth certificate for the groom dated Dukla, 2 Jul 1899, ref. no. 96/99, vol. I, p. 20, no. 37; the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate; with this addition, that the bride holds a trade licence for a cloth stall from the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno, of 18 May 1900, ref. no. 4369.
