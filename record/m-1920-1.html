      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      9 January 1920
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Kelman Tager, born in Klecie, merchant in Jasło, son of Leiba Tager, merchant in Jasło and the deceased Deichy née Zweig
      Bride
      Chaje Aron, born in Brzostek, no profession, daughter of Salamon Dawid, called Dawid, Aron, a merchant in Brzostek and Malka Perla, called Perla, née Tager
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Pinkas Korn, stallkeeper of cloth goods in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 21 Apr 1893, bride 14 Jul 1897. Remarks: For both the bride and the groom: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, pp. 13 and 54; and certificate of the banns, and certificate of the rabbinate; identity document as to the fulfilment of military service.
