      Marriage record 1 of 1910

      m-1910-1 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1910 · Number: 1

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1910

      Page scan: 1910_1-2_Bodner.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      11 January 1910
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Henoch Birnbaum was born in Brzostek, without profession, residing in Brzostek, the son of Kelman Birnbaum, a grain dealer in Brzostek, and Gittla, nee Plattner.
      Bride
      Feige Konigsdorf was born in Brzostek to her parents, the daughter of Samuel Mojzesz, called Mojzesz, Konigsdorf, a merchant in Brzostek, and Muszkieta nee Liebschutz
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lazar Silbermann, tanner in Brzostek, witness. Salaman Aron, owner of a cloth business in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 25 Sep 1885 bride born on 8 Dec 1889. Remarks: The capacity for the marriage entered alongside is attested by this registry's own birth book, namely for the groom vol. II, p. 169, no. 28, of [18]85, and for the bride vol. II, p. 251, no. 34, of [18]89; likewise the groom has shown that he has fulfilled the military obligation; further, the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate; with the signature of the bride's father, permitting this marriage. Samuel Mojżesz Königsdorf [signature, written at the top of the next entry's box].
