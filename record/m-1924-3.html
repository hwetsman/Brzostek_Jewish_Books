      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      29 March 1924
      Marriage Place
      Opacionka
      Groom
      Josef Sturm, son of Simche Sturm and Sara née Fenichel, born in Lubcza ad Jodlowa, single, no occupation, residing in Lubcza
      Bride
      Judesa Reis, no profession, in Opacionka, daughter of Hersch Reis, egg merchant and Scheindli née Lobel
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Wolf Töwel, grain dealer in Brzostek, witness. Lipa Rosner, synagogue attendant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 2 Dec 1893, bride 26 March 1897. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Jodłowa, 18 Aug 1919, ref. no. 44, [showing] that he is entered in the birth register there, vol. II, p. 175; and military service book of the District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) in Rzeszów, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 52, no. 9; and certificate of the banns, and certificates of the rabbinate.
