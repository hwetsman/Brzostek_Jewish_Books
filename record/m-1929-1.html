      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      15 January 1929
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Samuel Isaak, called Isaak, Nusbaum born in Baranów and lives in Brzostek, merchant, son of Jakob Nusbaum, merchant in Rozwadów and Chana nee Weinman
      Bride
      Freidla Licht, daughter of Lazar Licht, industrialist in Brzostek and Debora nee Plattner
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness. Josef Schwarz, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 11/dec 1895, brid 22 apr 1892. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Baranów, 21 Aug 1927, ref. no. 7[?], according to the register there, vol. I, p. 133, no. 62; certificate of domicile dated Rozwadów, 6 Sep 1927. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 3, no. 12; and notices of the rabbinate, and certificates of the banns.
