      m-1900-2 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1900 · Number: 2

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1900

      Page scan: 1900_1-2.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      25 February 1900
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Benzion Izaak, called Izaak, Rinder, born in Roza ad Radomysl, currently residing in Tarnów, unmarried, son of the deceased parents, Moses Rinder, a former innkeeper in Roza, and Estera nee Feld
      Bride
      Lea Konigsdorf, born in Brzostek to her unmarried parents, Feigi Konigsdorf and her illegitimate father, Moses Sperber, innkeepers living in Tarnów.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Naftali Silberman, owner of a tannery in Brzostek, witness. Abraham Plattner, owner of a spice stall in Brzostek, witness. Markus Dawid Hirsch, grain dealer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Documents for the groom: certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 3 Oct 1899, draft lot no. 282, that he has fulfilled his military obligations; certificate of the registry office in Radomyśl of 13 Feb 1898; certificate of the banns dated Tarnów, 10 Dec 1899, ref. no. 19; notice of the rabbinate here of 25 Feb 1900; these are kept in the register files. For the bride, this registry's own birth book attests, vol. I, p. 7, no. 64.
