      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      17 June 1930
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Daniel Katz, born and lives in Debica, no profession, illegitimate son of Itty Katz in Debica
      Bride
      Mindla, also known as Minnie, Siegfried, born in Brzostek, dauther of the deceased Leiser Siegfried, merchant in Brzostek and Chany Feigi nee Bochner
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Lipa Rosner, Synagogue attendant from Brzostek and Hersch March, cantor from Brzostek
      Notes
      Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Dębica, 4 Jun 1930, ref. no. 121/30; certificate of the banns dated Dębica, 4 Jun 1930, ref. no. 122. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 93, no. 24; certificate of the banns, and notices of the rabbinate. Below: I certify that in the first half of 1930 up to today [?] [number cut off] marriage records were entered in this duplicate [the rest runs off the bottom of the photo]; round seal.
