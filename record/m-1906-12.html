      Marriage record 12 of 1906

      m-1906-12 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1906 · Number: 12

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1906

      Page scan: 1906_10-12.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      7 March 1906
      Marriage Place
      Siedliska
      Groom
      Mojzesz Wrobel, false Propker, born in Lubatówka ad Dukla, Krosno district, cattle dealer, illegitimate son of Mendel Propker, propinator in Lubatówka and Kreindel nee Wrobel
      Bride
      Cypre Birn, born in Siedliska, daughter of Markus Birn, a propinator in Siedliska, and Beila, née Sperber
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Nechemie Storch m.p. [signed in the original], tavern keeper in Brzostek, witness. Markus Dawid Hirsch m.p. [signed in the original], tavern keeper in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Late entry made on the basis of the rescripts of the District Office (Starostwo) of 6 Mar 1908, ref. no. 1689, and of 28 Apr 1908, ref. no. 6562. Documents of the groom: birth certificate dated Dukla, 3 Feb 1906, no. 42; certificate of the banns of 4 Mar 1906, no. 71; military passport shown. For the bride, this registry's own birth book, vol. II, p. 193, no. 30, of 1886. The banns announced; the bride's father's consent to the marri[age; the rest is cut off at the edge of the photo]. In the bottom margin: I have entered [this] late (ref. no. 1065[1?]/912). In the officiant column: [struck through: Nechemie Storch m.p., tavern keeper[?] in Brzostek] corr. [initials, illegible].
