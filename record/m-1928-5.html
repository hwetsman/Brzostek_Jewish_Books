      ← Previous · Next → · All 1928

      Page scan: 1928_5.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      19 August 1928
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Chaskel Feiwel, called Feiwel, Bergman, born in Gorlice and lives in Jaslo, son of Hersch Bergman, merchant in Gorlice and Sprinza nee Guttman
      Bride
      Beila Ruchla, called Ruchla, Orner, daughter of the deceased Leib Orner, former owner of a habadashery shop in Brzostek and Ryfka nee Blasbalg
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Mendel Schüss, owner of real estate in Brzostek, witness. Hersch March, cantor in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      14 Feb 1899 and bride 15 aug 1895. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Gorlice, 2[3 or 8] Jul 1928, ref. no. 248, according to the register there, vol. IV[?], p. 115, no. 35; certificates of the banns dated Jasło, 19 Aug 1928; military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) Jasło, ref. no. 4171/3, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. 3, p. 36, no. 24; certificates of the banns; certificates of the rabbinate in Brzostek of 19 Aug 1928. Below entry 5: Closed as of 31 Dec 1928 [signature]; stamp: Jewish Registrar in Brzostek.
