      m-1905-3 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1905 · Number: 3

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1905

      Page scan: 1905_1-3_Leibel.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      13 August 1905
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Jozef Birnbaum, born in Brzostek, unemployed, residing in Brzostek house No. 79, son of Kelman and Gittla nee Plattner, industrialists in Brzostek
      Bride
      Lora Zahler, born in Brzostek, to Wilhelm and Maria née Leibel, industrialists in Brzostek
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Abraham Plattner, merchant in Brzostek, witness. Samuel Königsdorf, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Documents for the groom: as to his birth, this registry's own book attests, vol. II, pp. 105–6, no. 31, of [18]81; release from paternal authority by the Imperial-Royal District Court in Brzostek of 24 August [sic] 1905, Nc I 213/5; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 22 May 1905, ref. no. 34, that the military obligations have been fulfilled. For the bride, birth record, vol. II, pp. 199–200, no. 3, of [18]87; release from paternal authority by the Imperial-Royal District Court in Brzostek of 24 Aug 1905, ref. no. Nc I 214/5; the banns announced, and [the rest is cut off at the bottom of the photo].
