      18 June 1902
      Groom (prose)
      Mendel Schuss, grain merchant, born in Debica, residing in Brzostek, son of Aron David, called David, Schuss, a timber merchant in Debica, and Gela née Goldberg
      Bride (prose)
      Hinde Rubel, born in Brzostek, daughter of Isaac Rubel, a merchant and grain dealer in Brzostek, and her deceased mother Sarah née Freiberger

      Sarah Rubel née Freiberger, deceased.
