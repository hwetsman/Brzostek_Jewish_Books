      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      1 March 1900
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Chaim Majer, called Majer, Fisch, born in Frysztak, single, son of Elle Fisch, an egg merchant in Frysztak, and Temy nee Forgacz
      Bride
      Tyle Bieder, born in Brzostek, unmarried, parents Samuel Bieder, owner of a spice shop in Brzostek, and Feiga née Rubel.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Naftali Silberman, owner of a tannery in Brzostek, witness. Abraham Plattner, owner of a spice stall in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Birth certificate for the groom, vol. A, p. 25[?], no. 18, dated Frysztak, 3 Nov 1898, ref. no. 158[?], showing that he has fulfilled his military obligations; also the banns announced three times, and [the registrar's] own satisfaction that this marriage may be contracted, under § 32 of the register instructions.
