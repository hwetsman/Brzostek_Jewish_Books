      m-1900-3 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1900 · Number: 3

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1900

      Page scan: 1900_1-4.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      4 April 1900
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Jozef Silberbart, recte Silberberg, born in Pielnia ad Sanok, unemployed, son of Wolf Silberbart, recte Silberberg, innkeeper in Pielnia, and Sara Poper, residing there
      Bride
      Pesla Bloch, born in Brzostek, daughter of Aron Bloch, an industrialist from Brzostek, and Hena née Klotz
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Naftali Silberman, tanner in Brzostek, witness. Moses Klotz, owner of a spice stall in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Later note under the second copy (22 Mar 1928), on the basis of the Kraków Voivodeship rescript of 14 Feb 1928: the groom's surname is 'Silberbart', not Silberbartz recte Silberberg; he is the son of Wolf Silberbart and Sara née Proper. The same rescript authorised the late registration of his son Mendel's 1905 birth (b-1905-33), which names the father Józef Silberbart. The family otherwise used Silberberg. Remarks: Birth certificate dated Bukowsko, 8 Mar 1900, no. 55; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Sanok of 19 Jan 1899, ref. no. 1964/99, that he has fulfilled his military obligation; certificate of the banns dated Nowotaniec, 7 Mar 1900; notice of the rabbinate of 8 Apr 1900; for the groom, kept in the register files. For the bride, this registry's own birth book attests, vol. II, p. 29, no. 18, of [18]78.
