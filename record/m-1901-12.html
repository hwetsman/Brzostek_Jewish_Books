      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      24 October 1901
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom (prose)
      Simcha Joseph, called Joseph, Bodner, called Weintraub, born in Grudna Gorna, House No. 82, residing in Brzostek, industrialist, son of the ritual marriage of Ephraim Fishel, called Fishel, Weintraub, former innkeeper in Grudna Gorna and Mindla nee Berger
      Bride (prose)
      Esther Berger, called List, born in Brzeziny, Imperial-Royal, Starostwo Ropczyce, residing in Brzostek without employment, daughter of the ritual marriage of Aron List, a tavern keeper in Brzeziny, and Feige née Berger
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
