      Marriage record 4 of 1910

      m-1910-4 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1910 · Number: 4

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1910

      Page scan: 1910_3-4.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      22 July 1910
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Fischel Goldman, born in Nagawczyn ad Debica, without profession, son of the deceased Aron Goldman, former leaseholder of a toll in Nagawczyn ad Debica, and Tauba nee Drobner
      Bride
      Gittla Plattner, born in Brzostek to her parents, the daughter of Abraham Plattner, owner of a mixed-goods stall in Brzostek, and Alta Laja, called Laja, née Krebs.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Mozes Schwarz, owner of a mixed-goods stall in Brzostek, witness. Majer Löbel, leaseholder of the liquor concession in Januszkowice, witness.
      Notes
      groom born on 17 June 1884 and bride on 21 April 1887. Remarks: Documents of the groom: birth certificate, Dębica, year 1884, p. 134, no. 65; military passport as a reservist, and certificate of the banns dated Kraków, 5 Jun 1910, ref. no. 87. Also for the bride, this registry's own book, vol. II, pp. 205–6, no. 13; the father's consent to this marriage; the banns announced, and the notice of the rabbinate. Abraham Plattner [signature].
