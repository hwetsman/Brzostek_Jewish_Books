      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      9 July 1929
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Aron Lehrhaupt, born in Gorzyce near Dąbrowa Tarnowska, merchant, son of Chaim Peretz, called Peretz, Lehrhaupt in Gorzyce and Keili nee Schnikler
      Bride
      Mirla Breindla, called Breindla, Kornfeld, daughter of the deceased Fischel Kornfeld, former owner of property in Brzostek and Witla nee Karpf
      Rabbi
      Chaim Wolkenfeld (by proxy: Hersch March, cantor)
      Witnesses
      Samuel Salpeter, owner of a cloth[?] business in Brzostek, witness. Salamon Karpf, merchant in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      groom 20 march 1902, bride 24 jan 1898. Remarks: For the groom: birth certificate dated Dąbrowa, 1[8?] Nov 1923, according to the book there, vol. VII[?], p. 11; military service book, District Recruiting Command (P.K.U.) in Tarnów, of 5 Nov 1925, ref. no. [one character struck] 179, that he has fulfilled [his] military [duty]; certificate of domicile dated Gorzyce, 28 Aug 1928; also certificate of the banns dated Dąbrowa, 5 Jul 1929. For the bride: this registry's own birth book, vol. III, p. 58, no. 4; and notices of the rabbinate in Brzostek.
