      Marriage record 3 of 1911

      m-1911-3 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1911 · Number: 3

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1911

      Page scan: 1911_1-4_Offner.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      29 September 1911
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Abraham Feig, cattle dealer born in Laskówka Odporyszewska, residing in Błażkowa, son of Mordko Feig and Amalia Dekel, industrialists residing in Tarnów
      Bride
      Hinde Steinhorn, born in Błaszkowa, daughter of Jozef Steinhorn and Chaja Liba, called Liba, Walter, industrialists in Błaszkowa
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Mendel Dintenfass, leaseholder of the manor farm in Błażkowa. Salamon Freiberger, industrialist in Dzbany[?], witness.
      Notes
      groom born 5 oct 1878 and bride 7 dec 1887. Remarks: Birth certificate for the groom of the registry district in Dąbrowa, vol. IV, p. 117, no. 133; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Tarnów of 16 Feb 1905, ref. no. 58, of release from military obligation. For the bride, this registry's own book attests, vol. II, p. 105, no. 30; the banns announced; the notice of the rabbinate.
