      Marriage record 8 of 1907

      m-1907-8 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1907 · Number: 8

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1907

      Page scan: 1907_5-8.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      28 May 1907
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Efroim Handler, born in Makowiska ad Jasło, a merchant, the son of Juda Handler, a tenant of a propination plant in Makowiska and Scheindli, nee Trachman
      Bride
      Chaje Itte, called Itte, Schonwetter, born in Zawadka ad Brzostek, daughter of Efroim Fischl Schonwetter, owner of a farm in Brzostek and Złata née Wurzl
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Abraham Plattner, owner of a mixed-goods stall in Brzostek, witness. Moses Schwarz, grain dealer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Late entry made on the order of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 18 Jul[?] 1907, ref. no. 11473. Documents for the groom: birth record, Żmigród, vol. III, pp. 175–6, no. 55, of [18]81; certificate of the Imperial-Royal District Office in Eger (Cheb, Bohemia) of 20 Jun 1904 that the military obligations have been fulfilled. For the bride, this registry's own birth book attests, vol. II, pp. 183–4, no. 10, of [18]86; the banns announced, and the notice of the rabbinate.
