      Page scan: 1895_1-2_Israel_Offner.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      10 December 1895
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Jakob Rauch, born in Tarnów, in the suburb of Zabłocie, son of Eliasz and Jüdesa Rauch, unemployed in Tarnów
      Bride
      Golda Zanker, illegitimate daughter of Chaja Zanker, unemployed, in Brzostek
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Nechemje Storch, tavern keeper in Brzostek, witness. Wolf Töwel, owner of a spice stall in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Documents, namely: the groom's birth certificate dated Tarnów, 6 Nov 1892, ref. no. 693, vol. II, p. 58, no. 340 of the birth register there; also for the bride, the permission of the Imperial-Royal District Court in Brzostek, as the superior guardianship authority, of 8 Nov 1895, ref. no. 5256, to contract a legal marriage; kept in the register files; and the banns announced three times.
