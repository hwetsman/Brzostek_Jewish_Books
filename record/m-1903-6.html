      Marriage record 6 of 1903

      m-1903-6 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1903 · Number: 6

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1903

      Page scan: 1903_5-6.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      22 October 1903
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Süssel Winter, called Glasner, born in Piątkowa ad Nowy Sącz, residing in Blaszkowa, owner of real estate and trader, son of Leiba Glasner and Golda Winter, former innkeepers in Piątkowa
      Bride
      Matla Feilgut, called Kollender, born in Królówka ad Bochnia, residing in Błaszkowa, unemployed, daughter of Jakob Kollender and Chana Estera, called Ester, Feilgut
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness. Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer (Schächter) in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: The capacity [to contract, inserted above] the marriage entered alongside rests on [the registrar's] own knowledge, under § 32 of the register instructions; also the banns announced three times, and the notice of the rabbinate.
