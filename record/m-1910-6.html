      16 August 1910
      Marriage Place

      Groom (prose)
      Pinkas Chaim Herbstman, called Chaim and who used the surname Stierer, born in Leżajsk, no profession, son of Feige Herbstman, an industrialist in Leżajsk, and his illegitimate father Chiel Stierer, an industrialist there also
      Bride (prose)
      Rosa Hirsch, born in Brzostek to her parents, daughter of Marcus David, called David, Hirsch, a tavern keeper in Brzostek, and Hendela née Sperber
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
