      Groom (prose)
      Judah Zeughauser, born in Ryglice, Imperial-Royal district of Tarnow, innkeeper in Januszkowice and residing there, son of Moses Hersch Zeughauser, former lessee of the alcoholic beverages in Januszkowice and Zelda nee Glassner
      Bride (prose)
      Rachel Zeughauser, born in Wokowice, daughter of Feisel Zeughauser, a leaseholder of alcohol production in Wokowice, and Hannah Leah née Mahler.
