      Marriage record 8 of 1906

      m-1906-8 · Book: Marriage · Year: 1906 · Number: 8

      ← Previous · Next → · All 1906

      Page scan: 1906_7-9.jpg. Click to enlarge.

      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      30 November 1906
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Mojzesz Schyja, called Schyja, Eisen, known as Kirsch, born in Ropczyce, owner of a mixed-goods stall, residing in Januszkowice, son of Cypora Eisen, unemployed, residing in Ropczyce
      Bride
      Süsla Roth, called Unger, born in Frysztak, residing in Januszkowice, daughter of Chaja Sara, called Sara, Roth, unemployed, residing in Frysztak.
      Rabbi
      Judah Leib Wolkenfeld
      Witnesses
      Chaim Galizer, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness.
      Notes
      Remarks: Documents for the groom, namely: birth certificate dated Ropczyce, 2 May 1906, vol. IV, p. 125; decree of the Imperial-Royal District Court in Ropczyce of 20 Feb[?] 1906, ref. no. C. 257/6[?], declaring him of full age; notice of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Ropczyce of 12 Sep 1906, ref. no. 15448, of his release from military obligation. For the bride, birth record in Frysztak, vol. I, p. 75, no. 21, of [18]79; kept in the register files; the banns announced, and the notice of the rabbinate.
