      Transcription

      Marriage Date
      16 August 1914
      Marriage Place
      Brzostek
      Groom
      Izak Sturm, born in Januszkowice, merchant, son of Mojzesz Sturm and Estera née Roth, landowners in Januszkowice
      Bride
      Serla Heffner, illegitimate daughter of Syma Heffner, without a profession, in Januszkowice
      Witnesses
      Markus Kleinman, ritual slaughterer in Brzostek, witness. Kalman Hornik, merchant in Januszkowice, witness.
      Notes
      groom born 15 jan 1888, bride 7 jan 1885. Remarks: Documents of the groom: this registry's own birth book; permission of the Imperial-Royal District Office (Starostwo) in Pilzno of 5 Aug 1912, ref. no. 27. Documents of the bride: birth certificate dated Frysztak, 7 Aug 1912; certificate of the banns, and certificate of the rabbinate. Below the entry: Closed as of 31 Dec 1914. Wilhelm Zakl[?] [signature]; stamp: Jewish Registrar in Brzostek.
