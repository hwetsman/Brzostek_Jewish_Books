      Buildings

      The metrical books call them "houses" but in practice these are cadastral building numbers — the surveyor's parcel identifier for each structure in a town. The same number in two different towns refers to two completely different buildings, so every table below shows town and number together.

      Patterns to look for:

      • High event count + many distinct surnames at one building usually points to an institutional structure: a hospital, a synagogue, the mohel's residence, a school. (For example, in Brzostek many children were born and people died at Brzostek 1, while many naming events were held at Brzostek 149.)
      • One surname across many buildings usually points to a large extended family or a family that moved.
